[h2]Your uncle has left you his workshop. The town still remembers his floors.[/h2]

Mosaicus Maximus is a cozy craft and business game set in a small harbour town on the Bay of Naples in AD 72. Design mosaic floors for the townsfolk, lay them stone by stone, and restore the forgotten floors buried under the town. Start with a few sacks of black and white stone, and end with glass and gold for the temple of Venus.

[h2]Design it, lay it, reveal it[/h2]
[list]
[*]A client comes in with a wish: a guard dog and "Cave Canem" by the door, two doves for a bride-to-be, an octopus for the baths. Name your price and shake on it.
[*]Compose the floor at the design table from the pattern book's 31 Roman patterns and pictures and 9 borders. Drag, resize, mirror, pair and recolour them, and watch the stars it will earn as you work.
[*]Lay every tile yourself. Pick a stone from the tray, sweep the brush over the places that glow, and hundreds of tiles drop into place in a ripple, bending round curves the way Roman rows did.
[*]Wipe in the grout with a sponge, polish the stone with a cloth, then see the finished floor in the client's own room and hear what they think.
[/list]

[h2]Floors under the town[/h2]
Old floors turn up under houses being rebuilt. Scrape the dirt away with the trowel, mend each gap with a stone that matches, and the lost pattern goes into your pattern book. Some mornings your dog comes home covered in dirt, having found one next door. The digs grow all the way to the great floor beneath the forum.

[h2]Four chapters of Roman mosaic[/h2]
Every floor earns reputation, and reputation opens the next chapter: black and white geometry, then coloured marble and terracotta, then detailed pictures in tiny tiles, and finally glass and gold for walls, fountains and the temple. Rooms grow with you, from a small entrance hall to a villa dining hall and the forum.

[h2]A town full of people[/h2]
[list]
[*]Ten regular clients, each with a story that unfolds over their jobs, from Widow Cornelia and her guard dog to Trimalchio and his feasts, and twenty townsfolk with jobs of their own.
[*]Every client has a taste. Learn it from what they say, and your notebook keeps it.
[*]Copy famous floors for collectors, enter the town's floor contest every seventh day against the rival workshop, and lay themed floors for the Floralia, Neptunalia, Vinalia, Consualia and Saturnalia.
[*]Buy rare stone off the merchant ships in the harbour, make small picture panels at the workbench, and visit your floors on the town map.
[/list]
