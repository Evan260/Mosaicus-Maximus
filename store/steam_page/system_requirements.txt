WINDOWS

Minimum
OS: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
Processor: any dual-core processor from the last ten years
Memory: 2 GB RAM
Graphics: any graphics card or integrated graphics supporting OpenGL 3.3
Storage: 100 MB available space

Recommended
Memory: 4 GB RAM
Additional notes: The game is 2D and about 50 MB. The largest floors, of over ten thousand tiles, take a moment to set out on slower computers.


MACOS

Minimum
OS: macOS 13 Ventura or later on Apple silicon, or macOS 11 Big Sur or later on Intel
Processor: Apple silicon or a 64-bit Intel processor
Memory: 2 GB RAM
Graphics: any Mac that runs those versions
Storage: 250 MB available space

Recommended
Memory: 4 GB RAM
Additional notes: The game is 2D and about 190 MB, one app that runs natively on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.


LINUX / STEAMOS

Minimum
OS: a 64-bit distribution from the last five years, or SteamOS
Processor: any 64-bit dual-core processor from the last ten years
Memory: 2 GB RAM
Graphics: any graphics card or integrated graphics with a driver supporting OpenGL 3.3
Storage: 150 MB available space

Recommended
Memory: 4 GB RAM
Additional notes: The game is 2D and about 95 MB.
