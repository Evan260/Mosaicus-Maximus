MOSAICUS MAXIMUS - PLAY TEST

Thanks for trying this! The game is early, so expect rough edges.


STARTING IT

1. Put this folder anywhere, for example on your Desktop.
2. Double-click "Mosaicus Maximus.exe".
3. Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC". That is
   because the game is new and not yet signed. Click "More info", then
   "Run anyway".

Turn your sound on.


WHAT TO DO

Start a new workshop on the title screen: give your name and choose your
dog. It is AD 72, and you've inherited your uncle's mosaic workshop in a
harbour town on the Bay of Naples. Money is counted in denarii, the Roman
silver coin.

On your first day your uncle's notes appear on each screen, saying what to
do there, with an arrow at what to click. They stop after your first floor,
or press "Skip the guide" to turn them off.

- Click the job board to see today's notices, then pick a client.
- Agree a price with them by sliding the coins. Ask too much and they'll
  name their own price.
- At the design table, drag patterns and pictures from the book onto the
  floor plan. Drag a corner to resize one. Click any part of a pattern to
  pick it, then click a stone to colour it. Try to meet their wishes.
- Watch the stars on the design table: clients judge how the floor looks
  (symmetry, a centrepiece, how full it is, colours, a border) as well as
  their wishes. The hint under the stars says what to fix.
- A floor needs its stone in stock. Buy sacks at the stone shelves, or
  press "Buy it" on the design table to buy just what the floor is short of.
- Lay the floor one stone at a time: pick a stone in the tray (or press 1
  to 9), and sweep the left mouse button over the places that glow. Then
  pick the sponge to grout and the cloth to polish.
- See it finished, get paid, and keep the ones you like in your library
  (the scroll rack in the workshop). Then end the day.
- Every floor earns reputation. Enough of it opens the next chapter, with
  new stone, patterns and clients. Click the Chapter button at the top to
  see every chapter and what it brings.
- Achievements pop up as you earn them. The settings (the gear) list them.
- Between jobs, click the workbench (under the window, with the tools on
  the wall) to lay a small picture panel, one a day. Sell it there, or set it into a client's floor from the Pictures
  page of the pattern book: it comes laid already, and pays extra.
- Every tenth day is a festival. Its notices pay more, and the best
  festival floor wins a prize.
- Click a floor on the town map (through the street door) to see it again
  in its room.

Some jobs are old floors found under the town: clear the dirt with the
trowel, fill the gaps one tile at a time with matching stone (the chisel
lifts a wrong one; "Find a gap" shows you any you missed), press Done,
then grout and polish. Each one teaches you a new
pattern. Collectors ask for copies of famous floors, and every seventh day
there's a contest against the rival workshop.

Left drag      Lay tiles, wipe grout, polish, clear dirt
Right drag     Move around the floor
Mouse wheel    Zoom; on the design table, resize a pattern
R / F          Turn / mirror the chosen pattern on the design table
M              Add a mirrored copy on the other side
Delete         Take the chosen pattern off
Ctrl+Z / Y     Undo / redo on the design table
Esc            Leave a floor for later
F11            Full screen


WHAT TO TELL ME

- Was designing a floor fun? Was it clear what the client wanted?
- Did laying, grouting and polishing feel good, or drag on?
- Did you want to take another job after the first one?
- Anything confusing, slow or broken. A screenshot helps a lot: press
  Windows key + Shift + S, drag over the problem, then paste it into a
  message.
- Roughly how long you had played when you noticed each thing.


YOUR SAVES

Saved in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Mosaicus Maximus\
Floors you keep are in the game's library. Its "Copy to Pictures" button
puts a picture and a time-lapse in Pictures\Mosaicus Maximus.
