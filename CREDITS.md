The music is the free LITE edition of [Bard's Tales: Peaceful Harp Music Pack](https://mythagomusic.itch.io/bards-tales-peaceful-harp-music-pack-lite) by Amos Chab (Mythago Music). The fonts are Silkscreen by Jason Kottke and Fusion Pixel by TakWolf, both under the SIL Open Font Licence. Art was made with PixelLab; what each image cost is in `tools/art/ledger.csv`. The workshop's furniture was painted into the empty room with PixelLab's inpainting (`tools/art/make_workshop.sh`), then cut out piece by piece (`tools/art/make_workshop_layers.py`). The dogs, their breeds and their coats come from Farmus Maximus, the same studio's farm game.

The Kenney Impact Sounds pack in `tools/audio/downloads/` is CC0 and kept as a fallback. The game doesn't use it yet.
